# App Review Notes - Funky's 2P Games (1.50)

## Purpose and audience
Funky's 2P Games is a collection of 16 classic two-player games played face to face on ONE device (split screen, or turn by turn). It is for friends and families of all ages who want a quick game together. Free: no ads, no account, no in-app purchase, no data collection. It works fully offline and asks for no permission.

Games: Tennis, 4 in a Row, Pool, Memory, Word Clash, Air Hockey, Tank Wars, Carrom, Mini Golf, Star Clash, Caro, Awale, Duck-Day Chess, Hangman (shown as "Devine-moi", a French 5-letter word duel, when the device language is French), Reversi, Horse Jump.

## How to use the main features
No login required. No setup.
1. Launch the app: the game menu appears at once.
2. Tap any game tile to start it. Two players share the phone: in split-screen games (Tennis, Air Hockey, Star Clash, Horse Jump) each player touches their own half; in board games (4 in a Row, Caro, Reversi, Awale, Duck-Day Chess...) players tap in turn.
3. Tap "?" in any game to read its rules.
4. At the end of a game, tap the button to play again, or go back to the menu.
5. The music toggle is in the menu.

## External services
None. The app makes no network requests: all code, images, sounds, fonts and word lists are bundled in the app (it uses no analytics, ads, crash reporting, authentication, payment, cloud or AI service). Only local settings (music on/off, word-game language, recently used words) are stored on the device and are removed with the app.

## Regional differences
The app works the same in every region. The interface is in English, or in French when the device language is French (app title "Les jeux a deux de Pierre"; Hangman is replaced by the French word game "Devine-moi"). Word Clash offers French and English word lists in all regions.
