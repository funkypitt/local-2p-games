2P Games is a collection of 16 touch-friendly multiplayer games designed for two players. Share one device, with the screen split into two halves or each player in turn. Every game has built-in instructions. The app needs no connection and asks for no permission.

The games: Tennis (with power-ups), 4 in a Row, Pool, Memory, Word Clash, Air Hockey, Tank Wars, Carrom, Mini Golf, Star Clash, Caro, Awalé, Duck-Day Chess, Hangman, Reversi, and Horse Jump.

- 16 multiplayer games in one app
- Two players on a single device
- Works without any connection
- Calming background music with toggle
- Built-in instructions for every game
- No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases
