# Privacy policy — Funky's 2P Games / Les jeux à deux de Pierre

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

Funky's 2P Games (`com.local2p.games`) is published by Pierre Gallaz. It offers sixteen games for two players on one phone. It is free, has no ads, no account, no purchases and no tracking.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, embeds no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library, requests no permission, and never uses the network: everything it needs is inside the app.

## Data kept on the phone

Settings and scores stay in the app's private storage on the phone and are deleted with the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
